Odoo Shield Warden: money-at-risk controls for Odoo

ERP security is usually a checklist. Fraud is money leaving. I designed and built a controls engine for Odoo that stops risky vendor payments before they go out, puts a money figure on what was exposed, and keeps evidence an administrator cannot quietly rewrite.

The problem

Companies running Odoo protect accounts payable with role-based groups and the record chatter log. That leaves three blind spots.

The audit trail can be switched off

A caller with write access can change a vendor bank account while passing a context flag that disables change tracking. The payment run then pays the wrong account and the log shows nothing.

Checklists do not measure money

A hardening checklist says a setting is wrong. It does not say which payments were exposed or how much they were worth.

Evidence can be edited

An assessment stored as ordinary records can be rewritten by the same administrator it is meant to check.

What it does

Security and GRC controls

Continuous assessment mapped to ISO 27001, SOC 2 and CIS Controls v8: MFA coverage, login throttling, default admin checks, database manager exposure, weak database credentials, mass exports.

Anti-forensics monitors

Detects code stored in the database, custom record rules that hide data from auditors, and privilege drift against a sealed baseline.

Preventive payment hold

Payments to a new or recently changed vendor bank account are intercepted and need a second person to verify before funds are released.

Transaction monitoring

Rules for untracked bank swaps, bank accounts shared between vendors and staff, self-approved bills, duplicate vendor bills and rapid payouts to new vendors.

Money-at-risk cases

Related alerts are grouped into one investigation case per payment with a money total and a return-on-security-investment view.

Tamper-evident evidence

Every assessment and attestation is linked into a SHA-256 hash chain, with optional off-box seal receipts and an executive PDF scorecard.

How I proved it works

I wrote an attack lab that plays the fraudster and the insider against a local instance: bank swaps with tracking disabled, dormant server actions, record rules that blind an auditor, quiet privilege escalation. Then I ran review cycles until detection stopped missing.

Design decisions

Prevent, then detect

A hold on the payment beats an alert after the money left. Detection covers what the hold cannot see.

Cases, not alerts

One fraudulent payment trips two or three rules. Grouping by payment gives an investigator one case and one number.

Independent fingerprints

Bank details are hashed separately from the chatter log, so a swap with tracking disabled still shows as a mismatch.

Known limits, written down

Bank aliases re-pointed outside Odoo are invisible to ERP-layer controls. The documentation says so and recommends paying by account number.
